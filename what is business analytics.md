What is Business Analytics?

1 Business analytics uses data and statistical methods to understand past business performance.
2 It helps identify patterns, trends, and relationships in business data.

Roles of Business Analytics

1 Collect, clean, and prepare business data.
2 Analyze sales, customer, finance, marketing, and operational data.
3 Create dashboards and reports to visualize key performance iIdentify trends, risks, opportunities, and areas for improvement.
4 Build forecasting and predictive models for future business outcomes.
5 Use tools such as Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI, and Tableau.
6 Work with management and stakeholders to solve business problems.
7 Recommend data-driven strategies and measure their impact.
