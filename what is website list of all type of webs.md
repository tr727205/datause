. What is a Website?

A Website is a collection of web pages available on the internet under one domain name. It provides information through text, images, videos, and other content.

We can open websites using web browsers like Google Chrome, Firefox, and Microsoft Edge.

Examples: Google, YouTube, Facebook, Amazon.

2. Types of Websites
There are many types of websites. Some major types are:

1. Search Engine Websites: Used to search information on the internet.

Examples: Google, Bing, Yahoo.

2. Educational Websites: Provide online courses and study materials.

Examples: Coursera, Udemy, Khan Academy.

3. E-commerce Websites: Used for buying and selling products online.

Examples: Amazon, Flipkart, eBay.

4. Social Media Websites: Used to communicate and share photos and videos.

Examples: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn.

5. Business Websites: Provide company and business information.

Examples: Apple, Microsoft, Samsung.

6. Entertainment Websites: Provide movies, music, and videos.

Examples: YouTube, Netflix, Spotify.

7. News Websites: Provide national and international news.

Examples: BBC, CNN, NDTV.

8. Government Websites: Provide government information and online services.

Examples: india.gov.in, uidai.gov.in.

9. Banking Websites: Provide online banking services.

Examples: SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank.

10. Blog Websites: Share articles, opinions, and information.

Examples: Blogger, WordPress, Medium.

11. Job Websites: Help people find jobs.

Examples: Naukri.com, Indeed, LinkedIn.

12. Travel Websites: Help users book hotels, flights, and holidays.

Examples: MakeMyTrip, Booking.com.

13. Healthcare Websites: Provide health information and medical services.

Examples: Practo, WebMD.

14. Gaming Websites: Provide online games and gaming services.

Examples: Roblox, Steam.

15. Forum Websites: Allow users to ask questions and discuss topics.

Examples: Reddit, Quora.

3. Types of Websites Based on Structure

There are two main types:

1. Static Website:

A static website displays fixed content that usually changes only when manually updated.

Examples: Simple portfolio websites and basic company websites.

2. Dynamic Website:

A dynamic website displays content that can change according to user activity or database information.

Examples: Facebook, Instagram, Amazon.
