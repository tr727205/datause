LAN, MAN and WAN

1. What is LAN? (Local Area Network)

LAN is a network that connects computers and devices within a small geographical area, such as a home, school, office, or building.

Examples: School computer lab, home Wi-Fi network, office network.

Advantages:

High data transfer speed.

Easy file sharing.

Low installation cost.

2. What is MAN? (Metropolitan Area Network)

MAN is a network that connects multiple LANs across a city or a large geographical area.

Examples: City-wide cable TV network, university campuses across a city.

Advantages:

Covers a larger area than LAN.

Connects multiple local networks.

Supports communication across a city.

3. What is WAN? (Wide Area Network)

WAN is a network that connects computers and smaller networks over large geographical areas, such as countries and continents.

Examples: Internet, international bank networks, company networks across countries.

Advantages:

Covers large geographical areas.

Connects networks worldwide.

Supports long-distance communication.

4. Conclusion

LAN, MAN, and WAN are three major types of computer networks. LAN covers a small area, MAN covers a city, and WAN covers large geographical areas. All three help computers and devices communicate and share information.
